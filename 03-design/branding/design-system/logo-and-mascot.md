# Logo and mascot

ESTABLISHED: the name "Bear With Me" invites a character. Art direction: minimal, clever, rounded, expressive, recognizable, and adult-friendly, never a childish teddy. No logo has been chosen yet.

Everything below is NEW: three concepts plus a supporting paw. The marks are in the **Logos** asset group.

## Concept A: Kit Bear (recommended lead)

The bear's head *is* the organizer: a rounded pouch with two ears, and a zipper for a smile, with an orange pull. It says "this kit has your back" without a word.

- **Why it leads:** it ties the mascot to the physical product. The zipper pull becomes a real, ownable detail: an orange, bear-shaped or pill-shaped pull on the actual organizer.
- **Uses:** primary mark, guidebook cover, QR landing hero, organizer patch.
- **Watch:** at favicon size (16–32px), drop the zipper teeth and keep the ears, eyes and pull.

## Concept B: Wheel Bear

A round bear face inside a dark tire ring with dashed tread. It reads as "car" faster and works well as a round sticker or embossed circle.

- **Trade-off:** it is the most mascot-like and drifts closest to "cute." Better as a badge or sticker than as the primary mark.

## Concept C: B-Ears monogram

A heavy, condensed **B** with two round ears, on an orange rounded square. It is the most "brand" and least "character."

- **Uses:** app icon or home-screen shortcut, social avatar, embossed zipper pull, small packaging stamp.
- Pairs with either A or B as the compact mark.

## Paw detail

A simple four-toe paw for small touches: zipper pull, page-number markers in the guidebook, "you did it" checkmarks. Use one per surface at most.

## Wordmark (NEW)

**BEAR WITH ME** in Roboto Condensed ExtraBold, uppercase, tight tracking, in `ink`. **The Ready Kit** sits beneath it in Figtree SemiBold, sentence case, in `ink-muted` or `orange-ink`. Lock-ups:

- **Horizontal:** mark left, wordmark right, mark height = cap height × 2.
- **Stacked:** mark above, centered wordmark. Use it for the guidebook cover and organizer patch.
- **Clear space:** the height of one bear ear on every side.

## The bear as a guide (NEW)

- Appears at page edges, peeking from a corner, or beside a tip box. It never covers an instruction or a diagram.
- Has a small set of expressions only: neutral, "hmm" (one raised brow, for "not sure? start here"), and proud (for task completion).
- **Never appears on safety-critical content.** No bear on red-light or "stop" pages. Its absence signals that it's time to be serious.

## Next steps

1. Show A, B and C to 5 or more people in the target audience. Ask which feels "made for me" and which feels "for kids."
2. Test A at 24px and on the organizer fabric (embroidery loses fine detail).
3. Pick one primary mark and one compact mark.
