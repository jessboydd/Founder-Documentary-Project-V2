# User interviews — plan and notes

**Initial recruitment:** Women with limited experience in basic car care; seek varying driving backgrounds. Participation is voluntary.

## Neutral interview questions
1. Tell me about the last unexpected issue you had with your vehicle.
2. How did you figure out what to do?
3. What resources or tools did you have?
4. Which car-care task would you most like to understand?
5. What makes automotive instructions hard or easy to follow?
