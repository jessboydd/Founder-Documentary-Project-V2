# Usability testing — working plan

## Research question
Does connecting a colorful physical organizer and short illustrated guide to a QR-linked learning experience help beginners complete basic car-care tasks and make appropriate decisions?

## Scenario A — Tire pressure
In a safely parked or tabletop setting, observe tool retrieval, locating the vehicle's recommended cold tire pressure, following gauge steps, and understanding the reading.

## Scenario B — Dashboard warnings
Use simulated/photos of selected warnings. Observe how users locate guidance, describe or photograph a symbol, confirm any AI-suggested identification, and choose the next safe step using verified information.

## Suggested measures
- Time to find a tool / guidance
- Task-step accuracy
- Correctly identified warning and next step
- Assistance requests, hesitation, wrong guesses
- Confidence before and after (not proof of real-world safety)
- For experimental AI: symbol accuracy, uncertainty, unsupported claims, fallbacks
